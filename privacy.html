ClassPilot Free Preview Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 12, 2026.

ClassPilot Free Preview is a pilot classroom productivity tool. Classroom settings and instructional records created through ClassPilot are stored in a Google Sheet created in the signed-in user's Google account. Google account access is governed by the permissions shown on Google's authorization screen.

Data you enter

ClassPilot may store teacher-entered classroom settings, schedules, lesson pacing, instructional closeouts, resource links, school-closure dates, projects, and professional items in the teacher's ClassPilot data spreadsheet.

Student roster tool

The local roster used for the random student picker and group maker is designed to stay in that browser's local storage on that device rather than being written to the ClassPilot data spreadsheet. Users can clear the local roster from inside ClassPilot.

Third-party services

ClassPilot uses Google Apps Script and Google Sheets. If a teacher chooses to add a public YouTube school-announcement channel, ClassPilot may request that public source to find recent announcements. Google and YouTube have their own privacy terms.

Sale of data

ClassPilot Free Preview does not sell user data to advertisers.

Pilot use

This is a preview product intended for testing and feedback. Do not enter passwords, Social Security numbers, medical records, or other highly sensitive information into ClassPilot.
